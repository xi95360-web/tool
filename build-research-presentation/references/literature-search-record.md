## Coverage Checks

- [ ] Used multiple concept/keyword variants
- [ ] Used more than one independent database/library when accessible
- [ ] Traced decisive review claims to primary studies
- [ ] Searched for null, conflicting, and boundary-setting evidence
- [ ] Checked corrections, retractions, and peer-review status where relevant
- [ ] New searches no longer materially changed the claim map
- [ ] Limitations of access, language, dates, or indexing are documented

Do not call the search systematic unless a systematic-review protocol was followed. Otherwise describe it as a scoped, reproducible search.
